About

13 → 5

I design agentic AI platforms at Ascendion, where the work is taking capability engineers can already build and making it something the rest of an organisation can actually trust.

I came to product design from graphic design and motion, which is why I care as much about how an interface moves as how it's structured. Most of my work since has been on agentic AI at enterprise scale, where the hard problem isn't making the model work, it's making people trust what it produces.

The thread across my work is one problem in different shapes: the system does something genuinely useful, and nobody can tell what it did or why. Console, APB and Product Studio are three answers to it. I'm looking for a team where that problem is the main event rather than a side effect.

Sticks to timelines and delivers. A very dependable person, good at analysing, breaking a problem down to its root cause, and taking a structured approach.

My design director, Ascendion

How I work

  1. Break it to the root

    The audit for Console named four problems. Two of them turned out to be the same problem, which changed the fix from visual cleanup to consolidation.

  2. Structure before surface

    Most interface problems are structure problems wearing a visual disguise. Thirteen flat navigation items became five intentions.

  3. Prototype, then argue

    Build it, test it, change it. Disagreements settle faster against something real than against a description.

  4. Systemise and ship on time

    Solve it once, in a way four other teams can reuse, and land it when it was promised.
